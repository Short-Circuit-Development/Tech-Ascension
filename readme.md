# Mission
  This pack is aimed at the strong, the fearless, and the resilient. It is a progressive tech pack that takes you through different ages and timelines and includes some well-known content. This pack also combines these mods to streamline progression and boost immersion.
  
Do you want a challenge with incredible rewards waiting at the end? Do you want to push your creativity to the max? Do you want an experience that will encourage deep thinking? Then this pack is for you!

## Story
  You are a field researcher contracted by Exospace Exploration in an unknown sector spanning multiple galaxies. Your current assignment is to explore an uncharted planet, flagged for study and advancement. A pod from a transport ship carries you down through the atmosphere and drops you off, then returns. The ship leaves orbit, and you are alone. You carry nothing; no tools, no gear, and no rations. 

  NISI, your Neural Intelligence Software Interface, will guide you and determine the optimal steps for your research. This progression will be tracked by NISI and stored as data to be transmitted back across deep space for study by Exospace. They will use this information for the Inter-Dimensional Relay System (IDRS), a highway construction project to expand humanity’s influence between dimensions.

Survive. Study. Advance. 
**_Welcome to Tech Ascension._**

## Survival
  TerraFirmaCraft introduces a demanding yet rewarding survival experience that defines the foundation of Tech Ascension. The world is harsh, the wildlife is against you, and the environment itself becomes an obstacle you must overcome. 
  
  Progress begins only after your essential needs are secured: food, water, shelter, and warmth. Core survival systems include an expanded hunger and nutrition system, thirst, body temperature, and dynamic seasons. Understanding these mechanics ensures long‑term survival and leads to progression. Throughout your journey, the Neural Intelligence Software Interface (NISI) will serve as your guide, helping you adapt, survive, and advance through increasingly complex challenges.

## Technology
  Your technological progression in Tech Ascension begins at the most primitive level. Over time, you will advance through stone‑age tools, early metallurgy, steam power, electrical power, and advanced computer systems. As you progress, you will construct large machines, develop industrial‑scale factories, and automate complex production chains.
  
  Late‑game technology grants access to rare and exotic materials capable of powering hyper‑efficient manufacturing, planetary harvesting, and even stellar extraction. With these resources, you can build advanced transportation networks and infrastructure that redefine what is possible.

## Exploration
  Exploration in Tech Ascension is structured into distinct tiers, each representing a larger and more complex frontier:
  
    • Local — Your immediate world, traversed with tools, mounts, and jetpacks.
    • Micro‑Dimensions — Compact alternate realms such as the Nether, End, and similar mid‑tier dimensions, typically accessed through teleportation or advanced mobility.
    • Planets & Galaxies — Vast celestial bodies and star systems reachable only through spacecraft and atmospheric departure.
    • Dimensions — Boundless, reality‑breaking expanses accessible through specialized relays and extreme technology.
    
  At the pinnacle of "basic" exploration technology lies the Stargate, enabling instantaneous travel across known locations. However, even this marvel cannot breach true dimensions. Doing so would require manipulating singularities and bending spacetime itself; an endeavor most likely unreachable . . .  probably.
